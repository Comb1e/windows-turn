Keyboard identity examples

Build a new batch of about 20 photos across different scenes. Include visible bases, thin strips, and similar sofa/table/touchpad edges with no base. These identity labels are separate from angle calibration; no angle is required for absent or uncertain views.

For a visible base, choose Moving boundary and mark its two endpoints. Keep every photo from one capture group in the same role. Test photos and their duplicates must never become references.
